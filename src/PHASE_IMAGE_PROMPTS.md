# Phase Background Image Prompts

For use with Midjourney, DALL-E, Adobe Firefly, or similar. Each prompt is
built for a **vertical mobile-game map background** — painterly, warm,
illustrated, matching a Candy-Crush-map aesthetic but biblical in theme.
Not flat cartoon icons — a soft, atmospheric painted landscape a winding
path can sit on top of.

**Shared style suffix** — append this to every prompt below:
> "children's storybook game art style, soft painterly illustration, warm
> golden-hour lighting, gentle rolling terrain, no text, no characters in
> foreground, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, vibrant but not garish colors,
> Candy Crush map background aesthetic, high detail background scenery"

---

1. **The Birth and Early Life of Jesus** — A humble stable in Bethlehem at
   night, a bright star overhead, distant rolling hills of Judea, a small
   village with clay-roofed houses in the valley below, warm lantern light.

2. **Jesus' Baptism and Temptation** — The Jordan River winding through a
   golden desert landscape, distant wilderness mountains, sparse rocky
   terrain, a soft dawn sky.

3. **Jesus' Miracles and Healings** — A calm Sea of Galilee shoreline,
   fishing boats pulled up on sand, distant hillside town, soft clouds
   reflecting on the water.

4. **The Parables of Jesus** — Rolling wheat fields and vineyards under a
   wide open sky, a single sower's path through the fields, distant olive
   trees, pastoral daytime lighting.

5. **The Sermon on the Mount** — A gentle grassy hillside overlooking a
   valley and lake below, soft clouds, scattered wildflowers, peaceful
   morning light.

6. **Calling of the Disciples** — A rocky Galilean shoreline with fishing
   nets and small wooden boats, calm blue water, distant hills, early
   morning mist.

7. **Key Teachings of Jesus** — An ancient stone synagogue courtyard,
   olive trees, warm sandstone walls, soft afternoon sun.

8. **The Last Supper and Betrayal** — A quiet upper room window view over
   Jerusalem rooftops at dusk, warm candlelight glow, distant Mount of
   Olives silhouette.

9. **The Crucifixion and Death of Jesus** — Golgotha hill at dusk under a
   dramatic darkened sky, three distant crosses in silhouette, muted
   somber purples and deep oranges (keep respectful, non-graphic,
   background/landscape only).

10. **The Resurrection and Ascension** — A garden tomb at sunrise, soft
    golden light breaking through clouds, an open stone entrance, blooming
    garden greenery.

11. **Pentecost and the Holy Spirit** — Jerusalem rooftops under a
    dramatic sky with soft radiant light and gentle flame-like glow
    motifs, warm and awe-inspiring atmosphere.

12. **The Early Church Community** — A warm Mediterranean courtyard with
    stone buildings, shared tables, olive trees, soft daytime light,
    sense of community and gathering.

13. **Peter's Ministry and Miracles** — A coastal Mediterranean town with
    whitewashed and terracotta buildings on a hillside, a harbor with
    boats, bright clear daytime sky.

14. **The Conversion of Saul (Paul)** — The dusty road to Damascus, a
    bright radiant light breaking through the sky above a desert road,
    distant caravan route, dramatic golden light.

15. **Paul's First Missionary Journey** — A Mediterranean coastal cliffside
    with a distant sailing ship, ancient port town below, bright blue sea.

16. **Paul's Second Missionary Journey** — Rocky Greek coastline with
    olive groves, distant classical stone architecture on a hill, bright
    Aegean-blue water.

17. **The Jerusalem Council** — Jerusalem's ancient stone walls and
    rooftops from a distance, warm sandstone tones, clear daytime sky,
    sense of an important gathering place.

18. **Paul's Third Missionary Journey** — A bustling ancient Mediterranean
    port city skyline from the water, ships in harbor, warm late-afternoon
    light.

19. **Paul's Arrest and Imprisonment** — An ancient stone fortress/prison
    courtyard, heavy stone walls, a small barred window with warm light
    beyond, muted cool tones balanced with hope in the light source.

20. **The Spread of the Gospel to Rome** — A grand distant view of ancient
    Rome — aqueducts, classical architecture, warm sunset light spreading
    across the city, sense of a long journey's end and a new beginning.

---

**Naming convention when you save the files:**
`phase-1.jpg`, `phase-2.jpg`, ... `phase-20.jpg` — matches what
`phaseBackgrounds.js` expects.

**Recommended image specs:** at least 1080×1920px (portrait), JPG or WebP,
optimized/compressed for web (under ~300KB each ideally) so 20 of these
don't bloat your app's load time.
